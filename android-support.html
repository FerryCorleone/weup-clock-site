Android support

Set up an alarm

Tap +, choose the time, participant count, action, repeat days and ringtone, then save. Rehearse quietly to check framing before bedtime.

Android permissions and lock screen

Allow notifications, exact alarms, and full-screen alarm alerts when requested. Check lock-screen/banner/full-screen settings and manufacturer battery restrictions. The device and Android decide how a ringing alarm appears. Keep alarm volume audible; force-stop, shutdown, revoked permissions or system restrictions can interrupt reminders.

Complete a pose task

Use 2–4 people in the same camera view in good light. Keep the joints required by your upper-body or full-body action visible. Match the thumbnail together for 1.25 seconds. Move the phone to a safe stable position. This is not identity recognition or photo/video fraud prevention.

Cannot complete a task

Open task options -> Help -> Still unable to finish? -> End this task and confirm. This stops the task as an emergency, not a successful completion. Returning to the list keeps the task pending.

Photos, sounds and privacy

Custom actions are extracted locally. Microphone access is optional for recording your own ringtone. Completion photos are saved only when you tap Save image. No account, ads, analytics or cloud recognition. See the Android privacy policy.

Purchases and contact

The planned Android edition is a one-time paid Google Play download with no subscription or in-app purchases. Downloads and refunds follow Google Play rules. Support: qiezi.products@gmail.com. Include app version, Android/device model and the problem; do not send sensitive photos.

安卓帮助

设置闹钟

点击＋，选时间、人数、动作、重复和铃声，保存。睡前可无声演练，提前检查摆放与入镜范围。

安卓权限与锁屏

按提示允许通知、精确闹钟、锁屏全屏提醒，检查锁屏/横幅及厂商省电限制，保持闹钟音量。具体展示由Android和设备决定；强行停止、关机、撤权或系统限制可能中断提醒。

完成动作

2–4人同框，光线充足，露出半身/全身所需关节。对照缩略图，全员同时达标保持1.25秒。手机放稳，动作安全。不识别人是谁，不防照片视频模仿。

无法完成

任务菜单→帮助→仍然无法完成？→紧急结束并确认，记录为紧急停止。普通返回列表会保留未完成任务。

照片、铃声与隐私

自定义动作本地提取；主动录铃声才请求麦克风；合影只在点保存后写入相册。无账号、广告、统计或云识别，详见安卓隐私政策。

购买与支持

计划为Google Play一次性付费下载，无订阅、内购。下载和退款按Google Play规则办理。支持邮箱qiezi.products@gmail.com，请提供App版本、机型、安卓版本与问题，不要附敏感照片。
