Privacy policy

Effective: 13 October 2026 · Developer: Jin Luo

WeUp Clock is a local iPhone utility. We do not operate an app server, require an account, or integrate advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. We do not receive, sell, or upload your camera images, pose data, alarms, recordings, or completion records.

Camera and pose processing

The camera is used only in foreground action creation, rehearsal, and alarm tasks. Apple Vision processes body joints on-device. We do not identify faces or individuals, record camera video, or store live skeleton sequences. Pose detection is not identity verification and does not prevent imitation using a photo or video.

For a custom action, the app processes a photo you take or select through Apple’s photo picker. It stores only a normalized static pose template, its scope, and the name and hint you enter. The source image is released after extraction. Existing alarms may retain their own snapshot of a deleted action.

Optional completion photo

If enabled in Settings, the app temporarily keeps one completion photo and its pose overlay in memory. It is cleared when the result is closed or after 10 minutes. Switching to another app does not immediately discard that result. Nothing is saved to Photos automatically.

Only tapping Save image requests Photos add-only access and writes the result. The app does not browse your library. Saved images include participants, pose lines, target-pose thumbnail, participant count, duration, completion date/time, and WeUp Clock branding. Source EXIF and GPS metadata are not copied. Saved images are managed in Photos; obtain each participant’s permission before saving or sharing.

Microphone and local ringtones

Microphone permission is requested only when you actively record a ringtone. Audio is processed and stored on this iPhone. Unsaved drafts are deleted when you leave recording. Saved recordings can be reused by alarms and are not uploaded by the app.

Alarms, records, and preferences

Alarms, action snapshots, saved recordings, language and feedback preferences, and summary records are stored locally. Records retain at most 30 days or 100 entries. They contain no photos or camera video. You can clear records in Settings and delete items in their libraries. Deleting the app removes its local container; saved Photos images are separate.

iOS may include local app data in device backup or migration according to your Apple settings. Apple manages those system services; WeUp Clock does not provide its own cloud sync or backup server.

Permissions and control

Alarm access schedules system reminders. Camera access supports pose processing. The microphone is optional for recording. Photos add-only access is optional for saving a result. Photo import uses the system picker without full-library access. The app does not request contacts or location. You may change permissions in iOS Settings, which can disable the corresponding feature.

Support, diagnostics, and purchases

Diagnostics are local summaries unless you explicitly export them. If you email support, we receive the email and anything you choose to attach, and use it to answer your request. Please avoid sending sensitive media. App Store purchases, billing, and store analytics are handled by Apple under its policies; the app does not collect payment details or include in-app purchases.

Children and changes

WeUp Clock has no account, public feed, chat, or child-directed advertising. Ask a responsible adult to supervise camera use when needed. If data practices change, this policy will be updated and permission requested when required.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026年10月13日 · 开发者：Jin Luo

WeUp Clock 为本地 iPhone 工具，无账号、App 服务端、广告、统计或追踪 SDK。我们不接收、出售或上传相机画面、姿态、闹钟、录音及起床记录。

相机与动作

相机仅在创建动作、演练和动作任务前台使用。Apple Vision 在设备内识别身体节点，不识别人脸或人物身份，不录制相机视频，不保存运行时骨架序列。姿态识别不用于身份验证，也不防照片或视频模仿。

创建动作时，仅处理你拍摄或通过系统照片选择器选中的单张图片。保存标准化静态姿态模板、半身／全身范围、名称和提示；提取后释放源图。删除库条目不会改变既有闹钟中冻结的动作快照。

可选完成合影

开启完成合影后，内存最多保留一张照片与线框，退出结果页或十分钟到期即清除；切后台不会立即清除。不会自动写入相册。只有主动点“保存图片”才申请仅添加照片权限并保存，不浏览相册。

保存图包含人物、精细身体线条、目标缩略图、人数、耗时、完成日期时间及 WeUp Clock 品牌，不复制源 EXIF 或 GPS 元数据。保存后请在相册自行管理，保存或分享同伴照片前请取得其同意。

麦克风与铃声

仅主动录制铃声时请求麦克风。本机处理并保存音频，不上传；未保存草稿退出录制页即删除，已保存录音可复用于闹钟。

本地数据与备份

闹钟、动作快照、录音、语言与反馈偏好、简短记录保存在本机。记录最多保留最近三十天或一百条，不含照片或视频。可在设置清空记录、在库内删除条目；卸载清除 App 容器，相册中已保存的图片需另行管理。

系统备份和迁移可能根据你的 Apple 设置包含本地 App 数据，由 Apple 管理。App 不提供自己的云同步或备份服务器。

权限与支持

闹钟权限用于系统调度，相机用于姿态识别；录音和保存照片权限均按主动操作申请。导入通过系统选择器，不请求完整相册、通讯录或定位。可在系统设置撤销权限，对应功能将受限。

诊断仅在本地汇总，主动导出才离开设备。邮件联系支持时，我们收到你主动发送的邮件及附件，仅用于处理请求，请勿发送敏感媒体。商店购买、支付与商店统计由 Apple 按其政策处理，App 不收集支付资料且无内购。

儿童、变更及联系

无账号、公开内容、聊天或面向儿童的广告。儿童使用相机时应由负责的成年人陪同。若数据处理发生变化，将更新政策并按要求取得许可。
