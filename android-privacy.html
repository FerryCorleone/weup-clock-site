Android privacy policy

Policy version: 23 October 2026 · Developer: Jin Luo

WeUp Clock is an offline Android alarm and shared pose utility. This policy covers the Android app. The separate iPhone app policy remains at its existing URL.

Camera and pose processing

The camera is used only while you use foreground action creation, quiet rehearsal or an alarm task and grant camera access. Google MediaPipe and the bundled pose model process images on your Android device. We do not identify faces or individuals, record camera video, or store live skeleton sequences. Pose matching does not verify identity and does not prevent imitation using a photo or video.

Custom actions and selected photos

You may take a photo or select one through Android’s system Photo Picker or document picker. The app keeps only a normalized static pose template, its upper-body or full-body scope, and the name and hint you enter. Source images are released after extraction. The app does not request broad photo-library access. Existing alarms may retain their own action snapshot after you delete an action from the library.

Optional completion photo

If enabled, at most one completion photo and its pose overlay are kept in memory until you close the result or 10 minutes elapse. Nothing is automatically saved to your media library or sent to anyone. Only tapping Save image writes the result through Android MediaStore. The image includes participants, pose lines, the target-pose thumbnail, participant count, duration, completion date/time and WeUp Clock branding; source EXIF and GPS metadata are not copied. Saved images are managed separately in your device’s gallery and are not automatically removed when you uninstall. Obtain the participants’ permission before saving or sharing.

Microphone and recordings

Microphone access is requested only when you actively record a ringtone. Saved recordings and names stay in the app’s local storage. Unsaved drafts are deleted when you leave the recording flow. The app does not upload audio or listen in the background.

Alarms, records and preferences

Plans, action snapshots, recordings, language and feedback preferences, and task states are stored in the app’s private local storage. Summary records retain at most 30 days or 100 entries and contain no photos or video. You can clear records in Settings and delete supported items in their libraries. The app disables Android’s standard application backup and device-transfer rules and does not provide its own server backup or cloud synchronization. Separate operating-system or manufacturer services remain subject to their own settings and policies.

Permissions and control

Notifications, exact-alarm access, full-screen alarm alerts, the media-playback foreground service, vibration and wake locks support user-scheduled wake-up alerts. Boot/time-change broadcasts restore eligible schedules. Camera access is foreground only; the optional microphone is used for explicit recording. The app does not request contacts, location, advertising identifiers or broad storage/media-read access. You can revoke permissions in Android Settings; corresponding features may stop working. Android and the device manufacturer control force-stop, lock-screen alerts and background restrictions.

Data collection and sharing

The Android app has no INTERNET permission, account system, advertising, analytics/tracking SDK, developer server or cloud AI service. We do not receive, sell, transmit or share your camera images, normalized pose templates, recordings, plans or completion records. Local processing is not sent to MediaPipe servers. Developer test diagnostics are local aggregate timing/counts without camera pixels or raw joint coordinates; there is no automatic app log upload.

Support and Google Play purchases

If you independently email support, we receive the email and the information or attachments you choose to send and use them to respond. Please avoid sensitive media. Google Play handles app downloads, purchases, platform security and store reporting under Google’s policies. The app does not read payment-card details and has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Google’s platform processing is separate from the app’s offline operation.

Your controls and retention

Delete local library items and history in the app, revoke permissions, remove saved images in your gallery, or uninstall to remove the app’s local container. Existing plans can keep frozen action or ringtone references until those plans are deleted. There is no app account to delete. If you sent a support message, contact us using the same email to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable record-keeping requirements.

Children, safety and policy updates

The app is intended for shared wake-up use by friends, couples and roommates; it is not a child-directed service. It has no public feed, chat, account or targeted advertising. Use safe stationary poses with enough room and responsible supervision where appropriate. Do not rely on it as the sole reminder for safety-critical events. If processing practices change, we will update this policy and request permissions or consent when required.

Developer and data controller: Jin Luo. Privacy/support contact: qiezi.products@gmail.com.

安卓隐私政策

政策版本：2026年10月23日 · 开发者：Jin Luo

WeUp Clock为离线安卓闹钟与同框动作工具。本页适用于安卓App，iPhone版政策仍保留原地址。

相机与动作识别

相机仅在你授权并进入创建动作、无声演练或正式任务的前台页面时使用。Google MediaPipe及内置模型在这台安卓设备内处理图像，不识别人脸或人物身份、不录制视频、不保存实时骨架序列。动作匹配不用于身份验证，也不防照片或视频模仿。

自定义动作与导入照片

你可以拍摄或通过Android系统照片/文件选择器选一张照片，只保存标准化静态姿态、半身/全身范围、名称与提示，提取后释放源照片。不申请完整相册读取权限。删除动作库条目不会改变已保存闹钟中的冻结动作快照。

可选完成合影

开启后最多在内存保留一张完成合影及线框，退出结果页或十分钟后释放；不自动保存、不发送给任何人。只有主动点击“保存图片”才通过MediaStore写入设备媒体库。保存图含人物、精细线条、目标缩略图、人数、耗时、完成日期时间和WeUp Clock品牌，不复制源EXIF/GPS。已保存的图片由设备相册独立管理，卸载不会自动删除。保存或分享前请取得同伴同意。

麦克风与录音铃声

只有主动录制铃声才请求麦克风权限。已保存录音和名称保存在App本地，未保存草稿退出录制流程即清除。不上传音频、不在后台监听。

计划、记录与备份

闹钟、动作快照、录音、语言/反馈偏好和任务状态保存在App私有存储。起床汇总最多最近30天或100条，不含照片视频，可在设置清空。App已禁用Android标准应用备份及设备迁移规则，无自己的云同步/备份服务器；操作系统和厂商独立服务以其设置及政策为准。

权限与系统控制

通知、精确闹钟、锁屏全屏提醒、媒体播放前台服务、振动和唤醒用于用户设置的闹钟；开机和时间变化广播恢复有效计划。相机仅前台启用，麦克风仅主动录音。无通讯录、定位、广告ID或广泛媒体/存储读取权限。可在Android设置撤权，相关功能可能失效。强行停止、锁屏展示和后台限制由系统及厂商控制。

数据收集与共享

安卓App没有INTERNET权限、账号、广告、统计/追踪SDK、开发者服务器或云端AI。不接收、出售、传送或共享相机画面、静态动作模板、录音、计划和记录。本地识别不向MediaPipe服务器发图。开发测试诊断仅本地汇总耗时/数量，不含相机像素或原始关节坐标，也无自动日志上报。

支持与Google Play购买

主动使用邮件联系支持时，我们收到你选择发送的邮件及附件，用于回复请求；请避免敏感素材。Google Play按Google政策处理下载、购买、平台安全和商店报表，与App离线处理分开。App不读取银行卡或支付账户信息，无内购或订阅。

你的控制与保留

可删除库条目/记录、撤销权限、在相册删除导出图、卸载清除App容器。既有计划可保留动作或铃声的冻结引用直到对应计划删除。无App账号，无需注销。若曾发送支持邮件，可使用同一邮箱联系删除对应支持来信，依法需要保留的信息除外。

儿童、安全与更新

面向朋友、情侣、室友的共同起床场景，非面向儿童的服务，无公开内容、聊天或定向广告。保持安全站姿与足够空间，必要时由负责的成年人陪同。勿作为安全关键事件的唯一提醒。数据处理变更时将更新本政策并按要求取得许可。
