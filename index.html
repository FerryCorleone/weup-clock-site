FAQ

How many people do we need?

2–4 people in the same room, sharing one iPhone.

Can I still turn the alarm off?

Yes. iOS always provides its own Stop control on the lock screen, and WeUp Clock can’t remove it. An unfinished task can schedule a follow-up reminder. For critical events, set a backup alarm.

Do I need an account or internet?

No. There’s no account, no server and no cloud — all recognition happens on your iPhone.

Is the camera recording us?

No. The camera is only used during foreground tasks and no video is recorded. A completion photo is written to Photos only when you tap Save image.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone running iOS 26.1 or later.
