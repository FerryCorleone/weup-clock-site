Wake up together.

A shared pose challenge for 2–4 people.
One iPhone. One room. Your own morning.

WeUp Clock combines scheduled iOS alarms with a camera pose task. Match the selected pose together, then optionally save the moment. Recognition happens on your iPhone.

Support

Contact qiezi.products@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and a description of the issue. Do not send private photos, recordings, or passwords.

Set an alarm

Tap + on the home screen, choose a time, repeat days, 2–4 participants, a pose, and a sound. Save and allow alarm access when iOS asks. You can create and manage several alarms independently.

Complete the pose

Use the alarm’s task button and unlock normally. Allow camera access, place the phone securely, and get everyone in frame. Landscape gives groups more room. The thumbnail shows the target; dashed guides follow your body. Everyone needs to match simultaneously for 1.25 seconds.

Create your own action

Open pose selection and create an action. Use the five-second camera timer or import a group photo. Review the extracted upper-body or full-body pose, add a name and hint, and save. Full-body poses need visible knees and ankles.

Save a completion photo

After success, tap Save image. Photos permission is add-only; the app does not browse your photo library. Nothing is saved automatically. The temporary photo expires when you leave the result or after 10 minutes.

Troubleshooting

The pose is hard to match

Use good light. Keep each participant’s shoulders, elbows, and wrists visible; full-body actions also need knees and ankles. Avoid overlapping bodies and camera shake. Use quiet rehearsal before relying on a new setup.

The alarm is quiet

For bundled or recorded sounds, check the media volume and current audio output. System default sound is managed by iOS and cannot be previewed through the app. Alarm and haptic behavior can vary with permissions, system settings, and audio routes.

I need to end an unfinished task

Open task help, expand the unable-to-complete section, and confirm the emergency end. Closing the normal task screen does not count as success.

iOS owns the lock-screen Stop control. WeUp Clock cannot make an alarm impossible to dismiss. An unfinished task may schedule a follow-up when the app or its intent runs. Force-quitting, shutdown, revoked permissions, and system restrictions can interrupt this flow. Use another reminder for critical events.

Languages

Choose Language in Settings, or follow your iPhone’s language. English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, German, Brazilian Portuguese, and Arabic are supported. Your custom names and hints stay as you entered them.

设置和完成

首页点＋，选择时间、重复日期、人数、动作和铃声并保存。响铃后通过系统入口解锁进入动作任务，将手机放稳，让所有人入镜。横屏更适合并排站位。右上缩略图展示目标动作，虚线随身体引导；全员同时达标并保持 1.25 秒即可完成。

自定义动作与铃声

选动作时可用五秒延时拍摄或导入合影，本机提取后添加名称和提示。全身动作需要看见膝盖与脚踝。铃声列表点选即可试听内置或自录声音；系统默认声没有公开的直接试听接口。

照片与紧急结束

完成后主动点“保存图片”才申请仅添加照片权限并写入相册。结果页退出或十分钟到期后清除内存照片；不自动保存。无法完成时，可在任务帮助内展开“无法完成”区域，确认紧急结束。

系统锁屏“停止”由 iOS 提供，App 无法移除。未完成任务在 App 或其 Intent 获得运行机会时可安排再次提醒；强退、关机、撤权及系统限制可能中断。重要行程请准备额外提醒。
